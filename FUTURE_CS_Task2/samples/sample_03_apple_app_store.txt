TASK 2 – PHISHING EMAIL SAMPLE 03

Sample ID:
FUTURE-CS-T2-003

Source:
SeanWrightSec/phishing-examples
Public educational phishing example

Category:
Brand impersonation / purchase notification

Claimed Brand:
Apple / App Store

Message Theme:
The recipient receives a message concerning an App Store purchase and is
encouraged to interact with an attached PDF.

Suspicious Element:
The message uses brand impersonation and an unexpected attachment to
encourage the recipient to investigate a supposed transaction.

IMPORTANT:
This is a sanitized description of a publicly documented phishing
example. Original third-party content has not been copied into this
repository.

Attachment Safety:
The original attachment is not downloaded or opened as part of this
analysis.
